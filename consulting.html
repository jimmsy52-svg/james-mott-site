Thirty minutes on what is failing and what you have already tried. Free, and it is a real diagnosis rather than a sales call.

Weekly checkpoints with your team in the room. No black-box handover at the end.

Documented notebooks, a written summary, and a session where your team walks me through the model rather than the other way round.
