03 · Curriculum

Three sessions, three outputs

Session 1
3 hours

Find out what your model is actually doing

  • Diagnose the imbalanced class trap on your own confusion matrix
  • Separate accuracy from recall, and recall from business cost
  • Set a baseline you will measure everything else against
Outcome: You leave knowing why 94% accuracy caught zero defaults.
Session 2
3 hours

Build a model that survives a data refresh

  • Decision trees against random forests, on identical data
  • Class weights, threshold tuning, and SMOTE, compared side by side
  • XGBoost: what the extra recall costs you in false positives
Outcome: A ranked set of modeling choices with the trade-off written down.
Session 3
3 hours

Explain every prediction to someone who is not a modeler

  • SHAP waterfalls for high, medium, and low risk borrowers
  • Turning feature attributions into an adverse action notice
  • Anomaly detection when you have no target variable at all
Outcome: A model you can put in front of a regulator or a credit committee.
