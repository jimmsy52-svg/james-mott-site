# Jim Mott — Brand Guide

## Brand Identity in Three Words

Precise. Credible. Practitioner.

## Visual Style Principles

The brand feels like a working professional's desk, not a startup's pitch deck.  
Editorial and clean, with enough warmth to signal accessibility.  
Technical credibility is visual too: charts and code are presented clearly, not decorated.  
No gradients for the sake of gradients. No stock-photo aesthetics.  
Data visualizations should be readable first, styled second.

## Typography

*Finalize with specific font names when ready.*

- **Headings:** Clean, modern serif or strong sans-serif (e.g., Georgia, Merriweather, or Inter Bold)
- **Body:** Readable sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Source Sans, or similar)
- Avoid display fonts that read as decorative or trendy
