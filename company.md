# Jim Mott — Company / Professional Context

## What I Do

I'm an independent data science educator, curriculum developer, and practitioner specializing in fraud detection, machine learning, and applied AI. I operate across three channels: direct instruction at universities and learning platforms, practitioner/analyst work in fraud and risk domains, and a LinkedIn content presence targeting working data scientists.

## Mission

To turn vague AI/ML awareness into immediate, practical clarity. Not theoretical familiarity. Actual skill that people can apply on the job, with their own data, starting now.

## Who I Serve

**Learners and practitioners:**  
Data scientists, ML practitioners, fraud and risk analysts, and professionals transitioning into applied AI roles. They know enough to be dangerous but want to go deeper — especially in areas where the stakes are real (fraud, risk, financial modeling).

**Organizations:**  
Teams or institutions that need both the technical expertise *and* the instructional infrastructure to build internal capability in fraud detection and risk modeling. The dual value: I can do the work and teach others to do it.

**LinkedIn audience:**  
Working data scientists and ML practitioners who want practitioner-level insights grounded in real datasets, not recycled LinkedIn content or AI hype.

## What I Teach and Build

**Core technical topics:**
- Fraud detection and risk modeling
- Machine learning (classification, ensemble methods, decision trees, random forests)
- Imbalanced class handling
- SHAP / model interpretability
- Bayesian analysis
- Latent Class Analysis (LCA)
- Conditional inference trees (SPSS STATS CITREE)
- VIF / multicollinearity diagnostics
- Applied GenAI

**Datasets used in tutorials:** LendingClub loan data (primary public dataset for practitioner examples)

**Teaching philosophy:** Real data. Explain *why*, not just *how*. Immediate applicability over theoretical completeness.
